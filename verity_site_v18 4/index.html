What we actually measure

Every result traces back to a specific, well-studied marker — never a black box.

Speech pace & pauses

How quickly words come, and how much silence sits between them — one of the most consistent findings in cognitive aging research.

Vocal energy variability

How much your loudness varies through a sample, adjusted so recording quality doesn't skew the reading.

Writing rhythm

Stroke timing and hesitation while writing, captured without judging handwriting quality.
